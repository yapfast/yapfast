Most icons come from Solar, the icon set by 480 Design
(https://www.figma.com/community/file/1166831539721848736), under CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). They were taken from Iconify's
copy of the set (https://icon-sets.iconify.design/solar/) on 2026-10-23, in its
Bold style unless noted, and run through svgo, which rounds coordinates to two
decimals. Filled icons are the rule, as in Discord; line icons are kept for
glyphs such as check, x and chevrons.

- From Solar, unchanged: at-sign (mention-circle), ban (forbidden-circle),
  bell, bell-off, chart-bar (chart), circle-plus (add-circle), circle-stop
  (stop-circle), clipboard-paste (clipboard), clock (clock-circle), copy,
  copy-plus (copy-add), crown, download (download-minimalistic), ellipsis
  (menu-dots), external-link (square-arrow-right-up), eye, file, folder,
  folder-plus (add-folder), forward (forward-right), gavel (sledgehammer),
  headphones (headphones-round), history, image (gallery), inbox, link, lock,
  log-out (logout-2), message-circle (chat-round), message-square-text
  (chat-square), palette (palette-2), pause, pencil (pen), phone, phone-off
  (end-call), pin, play, refresh-cw (refresh), scissors, send-horizontal
  (plain), settings, shield, smile and smile-plus (smile-circle), square-pen
  (pen-new-square), star (star, Linear style), star-filled (star), sticky-note
  (notes), text-select (text-selection), trash-2 (trash-bin-trash),
  trending-up (graph-up), triangle-alert (danger-triangle), user, users
  (users-group-rounded) and video (videocamera).
- Adapted from Solar: headphone-off, eye-off, pin-off and video-off are the
  icon of the same name with a slash, and a gap cut around it; screen-share is
  Solar's monitor with an arrow cut out of its screen, and screen-share-off
  that monitor with the slash; reply is forward mirrored; search is the
  Linear magnifier with a 2-unit stroke.
- Drawn for Yapfast in Solar's manner: gif, hash, mic and mic-off, the
  signal-high, signal-medium and signal-low bars, soundboard (the speaker of
  volume-2 with two sparkles), thread (a spool of thread,
  as Discord marks threads), user-minus, user-pen, user-plus and user-x, and
  the speaker of volume-2, volume-2-bold and volume-x.

The rest are Lucide icons (https://lucide.dev/), under the accompanying
LICENSE (ISC): check, chevron-down, chevron-left, chevron-right, maximize,
minimize, minus, restore, slash, square and x. restore is Lucide's copy icon,
which the title bar shows for its restore button.

discord.svg is Discord's logo from Simple Icons 16.34.0
(https://simpleicons.org/, CC0 1.0), filled white. It marks Discord servers
and DMs, and Discord's brand guidelines apply to it rather than the ISC licence.

logo.svg is the app's own mark, the Shout balloon from the Yapfast logo
sketches: a comic shout balloon with typing dots cut out of it.

scroll-up, scroll-down, scroll-left and scroll-right are the scrollbar arrows of
Slint 1.18.1's Fluent style (https://slint.dev/), unmodified, under Slint's
licence (GPL-3.0-only, the Royalty-free License 2.0 or the Software License 3.0).
